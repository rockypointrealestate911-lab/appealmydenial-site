Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

The short version. Your letter is read and then thrown away. The details from it are saved on your phone, not on our servers. We send your letter to OpenAI to read it only after you say yes. We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

Appeal My Denial is an iPhone app that reads a health insurance denial letter, explains why the claim was denied and when your appeal is due, and drafts an appeal letter for you to review and send. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and how to delete it.

What the app handles

  • Your denial letter. The PDF, scan, or photos you choose, up to five pages. If a PDF page has readable text, the app sends only that text. Otherwise it sends an image of the page.
  • Details read from the letter. For example: insurer name, plan type, member ID, claim or reference number, service, procedure and denial codes, dates, the reason given, the appeal deadline and address, provider name, and amount billed. You can review and correct every one of them.
  • What you type. Corrections, your name and address for the letter, the date the letter arrived, and anything you add to a draft.
  • Your saved appeals and drafts. These stay on your phone.
  • Purchase status. Apple handles payment. Our payments provider, RevenueCat, tells the app whether you have bought an appeal or a subscription, using a random app ID. We never see your card details.
  • Basic connection data. Like any app that connects to the internet, our server and its hosting provider receive technical information such as your IP address and the time of the request, so the request can be delivered and the service kept running.

The app does not ask you to create an account. It does not use advertising, tracking, or analytics tools inside the app.

Where your letter goes

When you ask the app to read a letter, it is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to OpenAI, an artificial intelligence company in the United States, to pull out the details listed above. The details come back to your phone.

  • This happens only after you tap Agree on the consent screen the first time. If you tap Not now, nothing is sent and you can type the details yourself.
  • Our server does not save your letter, its images, or the details read from it, and is built not to log them.
  • We send requests to OpenAI with the setting that asks OpenAI not to store them for later use. OpenAI's handling of data sent through its API is governed by OpenAI's privacy policy, which allows it to keep API data for a limited time to monitor for abuse.
  • Your saved appeals and drafts are not sent to OpenAI or to us.

Sample letters inside the app are fictional and are never sent anywhere.

Things you choose to share

The app can add a deadline reminder to your calendar, and lets you copy, email, print, or share a letter. These go wherever you send them, using your phone's own apps. We do not receive copies.

This website

Our website, appealmydenial.app, uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It does not use cookies, does not track you across other sites, and does not collect personal information. The deadline calculator runs entirely in your browser; the dates you enter are not sent to us or anyone else.

How we use information

  • To read your letter, explain the denial, work out your deadline, and draft your letters.
  • To confirm purchases.
  • To keep the service working and secure.

We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or use your letters to train AI models.

How long information is kept

Letters and the details read from them are not kept on our servers after the reading is done. Saved appeals stay on your phone until you delete them or delete the app. Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as needed to provide your purchases.

Deleting your information

Open Settings in the app and tap Delete all my data. This removes your saved appeals and drafts from your phone and resets your consent. You can also delete a single appeal from its screen. Copies you already emailed, printed, shared, or added to your calendar are not affected, so remove those where you saved them. You do not need to contact us to delete your data.

Health information

Denial letters contain health information. Appeal My Denial is not a health plan or health care provider. We treat your letter as sensitive: it is read only with your consent, it is not stored on our servers, and it is not used for anything except reading your letter for you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. A parent or guardian can use the app to appeal a denial on a child's behalf.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. No method of sending or storing information is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Because we do not keep your letters or saved appeals on our servers, most of that information is on your phone and under your control. For anything else, email us.

Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version here with a new date. If a change affects how your letter is handled, the app will ask for your consent again.
