External review in Maine

Checked against official sources October 4, 2026

Short answer: If your health plan upheld its denial after your internal appeal, you can ask an independent reviewer to decide. In Maine, you send the request to the state agency below.

At a glance

Who runs itMaine Bureau of Insurance (contracts External Review Organizations)
Deadline to request12 months after the plan's final decision.
Where to startOfficial request page or form
Phone207-624-8475 / 800-300-5000 (ME only)
FeeNo fee listed. Most states charge nothing.
Fast (expedited) reviewAvailable when waiting could seriously harm your health. Ask for it when you file.

Before you file

  • Finish the plan's internal appeal first, unless your situation is urgent. How to appeal
  • Check which rules apply. State programs cover plans the state regulates. Self-funded employer plans usually use an independent reviewer the plan hires under federal rules, and Medicare and Medicaid have their own appeals. Your final denial letter says which.
  • Send everything: the final denial, your internal appeal, and your doctor's letter.

Details change. Confirm with the agency before you file, and go by your denial letter if it gives a different deadline.
