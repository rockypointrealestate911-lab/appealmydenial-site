How the deadlines work

  • Medicare Advantage: 65 days from the date of the notice. Guide
  • Original Medicare: 120 days from receiving the Medicare Summary Notice, presumed to be 5 days after its date. Guide
  • Marketplace and job-based plans: 180 days from receiving the denial. Marketplace guide · Job-based guide
  • Medicaid health plans: 60 days from the date on the notice. To keep a service you already get during the appeal, file within 10 days of when the notice was sent or by the date the change takes effect, whichever is later. Guide

If waiting could seriously harm your health, don't wait for the deadline: call your plan today and ask for an expedited (72-hour) appeal. On Medicare and being discharged from the hospital, or your skilled nursing, home health or rehab care is ending? Call the Quality Improvement Organization (QIO) on your notice right away, before you leave the hospital.
