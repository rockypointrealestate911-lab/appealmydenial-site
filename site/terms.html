What the app does, and does not do

Appeal My Denial reads a health insurance denial letter, explains in plain words why the claim was denied, works out your appeal deadline from the rules for your type of plan, and drafts letters for you to review, edit, and send yourself. It can also point you to the next level of review, such as your state's independent external review program.

Not legal, medical, or insurance advice. Appeal My Denial is not a law firm, a lawyer, a doctor, or an insurance company, and using it does not create an attorney-client or any other professional relationship. The letters are drafts based on the information you provide. You decide whether and what to send. For free help, contact your State Health Insurance Assistance Program (for Medicare) or your state insurance department's consumer line.

Deadlines are your responsibility

The app shows the deadline it calculated and the rule it used. If your letter states its own deadline, the app shows both and uses the earlier date. Insurance rules vary and can change, and the app can misread a letter. Always check the deadline printed on your letter and send your appeal before it. Using the app does not pause or extend any deadline. If your health could be seriously harmed by waiting, call your plan today and ask for an expedited appeal.

Accuracy

Reading letters with artificial intelligence can be imperfect, especially from photos. Check every detail on the review screen against your letter before you continue. Information about state review programs is checked against official sources but can go out of date; confirm it with the agency before you file.
