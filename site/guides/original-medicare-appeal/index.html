How to appeal an Original Medicare denial

Reviewed October 4, 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer: Ask for a redetermination within 120 days of receiving your Medicare Summary Notice. Medicare assumes you got it 5 days after its date, so count 125 days from the date printed on it. The contractor usually decides within 60 days.

Where the denial shows up

Original Medicare tells you about denied claims on your Medicare Summary Notice (MSN), which comes every few months, or sooner on Medicare.gov. Find the claim, the reason, and the date of the notice.

Your deadline: 120 days from receipt

You have 120 days from the day you receive the MSN. Unless you can show otherwise, Medicare presumes you received it 5 days after the date on it. Counting 125 days from the printed date keeps you safe. Calculate it here.

How to file a redetermination

  1. Circle the denied item on your MSN, write why you disagree, sign it, and send a copy to the contractor address on the MSN. Or use form CMS-20027, or a letter.
  2. Include your name, Medicare number, the item or service, the dates of service, and why you think it should be paid.
  3. Add any records that support you, such as a letter from your doctor.
  4. Keep copies of everything.

What happens next

  • Level 1, redetermination: the Medicare Administrative Contractor usually decides within 60 days.
  • Level 2, reconsideration: if you disagree, ask a Qualified Independent Contractor within 180 days of the redetermination decision.
  • Further levels include an administrative law judge hearing for cases above a set dollar amount.

Free help

Your State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) offers free, one-on-one Medicare counseling.

Common questions

How long do I have to appeal an Original Medicare claim?

120 days from the day you receive the Medicare Summary Notice. Medicare presumes you received it 5 days after its date.

What is a redetermination?

It's the first level of appeal in Original Medicare: a new look at your claim by the Medicare Administrative Contractor that processed it.
