How to appeal a Marketplace (ACA) plan denial

Reviewed October 4, 2026 · 6 min read

Short answer: File an internal appeal with your insurer within 180 days of receiving the denial. If the insurer says no again, you can ask for an independent external review, and that decision is binding on the insurer.

Why appeal

In 2024, Marketplace insurers on HealthCare.gov denied about 19% of in-network claims, yet consumers appealed fewer than 1% of the denials. Insurers upheld 66% of appealed denials, so about a third were not upheld at that first step. External review is a separate chance after that.

Step 1: Internal appeal, within 180 days

You have 180 days from receiving the denial notice to ask your insurer to reconsider. Check your date. The insurer must decide:

  • Urgent care: within 72 hours.
  • Service not yet received: within 30 days.
  • Service already received: within 60 days.

You have the right to see your claim file and the documents the insurer relied on, free of charge, and to submit your own evidence.

Step 2: External review

If the internal appeal fails, you can ask for an external review by an independent reviewer. Depending on your state, it's run by the state or by the federal process. You generally have 4 months from the final denial, though some states allow longer. Find your state.

If your situation is urgent, you can ask for an expedited external review at the same time as an urgent internal appeal.

What to send

  • A short letter naming the claim, quoting the reason, and answering it.
  • Your doctor's letter and records for medical necessity denials. How to answer that reason.
  • A copy of the denial and of your insurance card.

Common questions

How long do I have to appeal a Marketplace plan denial?

180 days from receiving the denial notice for the internal appeal. After a final denial, you generally have 4 months to request external review, longer in some states.

Is the external review decision final?

If the external reviewer overturns the denial, the insurer must accept it and pay or cover the service.
